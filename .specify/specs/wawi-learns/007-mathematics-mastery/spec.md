# SLC-007 — Mathematics mastery specification

**Requirements:** PRD-FR-016
**Depends on:** SLC-006

## Vertical scope

Add Reception and Year 1 mathematics to the same profile, scheduler, mastery, rewards, offline stores and parent evidence. New concepts move concrete → pictorial → abstract, begin with worked examples, classify misconceptions and require varied delayed evidence without speed pressure.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-SLC-007-001:** Maths skills, representations, misconception tags and dimension-specific mastery integrate without changing English evidence.
- **AC-SLC-007-002:** Every Reception outcome in PRD FR-MATH-01 has an offline worked-example and practice journey.
- **AC-SLC-007-003:** Every Year 1 outcome in FR-MATH-02 has an offline worked-example and practice journey.
- **AC-SLC-007-004:** Representative skills progress through CPA forms, adapt to misconceptions and reach mastery only through varied representation and delayed recall.

## Non-goals

No Year 2 content, speed-based negative feedback, unsafe free randomisation, isolated maths profile or separate reward system.
