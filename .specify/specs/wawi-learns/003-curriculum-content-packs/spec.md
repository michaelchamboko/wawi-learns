# SLC-003 — Curriculum and content packs specification

**Requirements:** PRD-FR-001, PRD-FR-002, PRD-FR-019, PRD-FR-027, PRD-NFR-008
**Depends on:** SLC-002

## Vertical scope

Produce a complete, human-reviewed, versioned Reception/Year 1 core syllabus that builds into reproducible essential/full offline packs and is impossible to publish when schema, curriculum, safety, answer, asset, licence or integrity validation fails.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-SLC-003-001:** Versioned Zod schemas represent every PRD §12 content field and reject incompatible or incomplete records.
- **AC-SLC-003-002:** The reviewed core contains at least 2,000 British-English word records, 800 illustrated concrete/action words, a complete GPC inventory and reviewed isolated phoneme audio.
- **AC-SLC-003-003:** The core contains at least 400 decodable phrases/sentences, 60 levelled stories, reviewed comprehension keys, images, tracing assets and licence provenance.
- **AC-SLC-003-004:** Mathematics covers every Reception/Year 1 strand with at least 40 validated reusable templates, manipulatives/diagrams and exact answer logic.
- **AC-SLC-003-005:** Essential/full packs are hashed, version-compatible and reproducible; any validation failure blocks publication and leaves the prior pack active.

## Non-goals

No runtime CMS, admin role, AI-generated item counted as curated without promotion, proprietary phonics-programme copying or user-facing content authoring.
